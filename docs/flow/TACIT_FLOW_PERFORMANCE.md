# Performance and cost — measured versus unmeasured

Original twelve successful Flash-Lite requests (synthetic text, one at a time): wall latency p50 **1,102 ms**, p95 **1,684 ms**, nearest-rank, n=12. The new eight-case Flash-Lite stress set spans 918–1,848 ms (p50 1,002 ms, p95 1,848 ms). Semantic quality did not meet target; low latency is not useful-result proof. Eight 3.8 Flash LOW requests reached the 5 s deadline. Successful-response usage/cost and missing abort billing are in TEST_REPORT/evidence.

Persistent local CPU whisper-server: synthetic TTS files of 1.67, 2.38 and 2.82 s decoded in **618, 612, 610 ms**. First two outputs normalized spoken four/five to numerals; the third omitted “The” and formatted sixty dollars as `$60`. These are not lossless verbatim recordings. No real-call WER, speech-end timestamp or capture→card latency exists. Do not sum these numbers and advertise a measured end-to-end latency.

Engineering bounds, not heap measurements: PCM 32,000 B/s; 100 ms frames; extension transport backlog cap 32 KB; max local VAD segment 10 s plus bounded endpoint/pre-roll; one local decode + one pending job; 24×2,000-character source turns; six anchors; twelve relationships; three 500-character drafts. Model sees at most twelve finalized turns, one request active, 8/min and 80/server lifetime, 1,800 maximum output tokens. Partial local hypotheses start after roughly 1.5 s of collected speech; only finals get semantic interpretation. Coalescing adds up to 700 ms after final transcript. Receipt timestamps are not source acoustic timestamps.

No concurrent Auto-AVSR capture/STT CPU/GPU/memory profile has been measured. Dedicated local STT runs CPU four threads to avoid adding another Metal decoder; that is an implementation choice, not proof of acceptable contention. Profile fifteen minutes with actual camera/Meet once permissions/participants are available, record peaks/p50/p95 and all gaps. No speedup or energy claim is justified by idle process presence.
