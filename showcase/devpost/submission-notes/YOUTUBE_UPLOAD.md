## Title (≤100 chars)
TACIT — Turning Silent Articulation Into Speech | HackUMass XIV

## Description
TACIT is assistive communication for people who can't speak aloud. You mouth your words silently to your Mac's camera. TACIT reads your lips with an on-device visual speech recognition model, shows you the words first, and speaks only what you approve, in the voice you choose, inside an ordinary call.

What's in the demo
• Silent articulation → recognized words (real recordings, video only, no audio)
• Review first: edit any word; nothing is spoken until you press Speak; Stop cancels instantly
• The Living Notch: a native macOS notch app (compact, hover Peek, expanded workspace)
• My Voice: pick and preview an installed macOS voice
• Hold my turn and saved phrases
• Help Me Respond: Google Gemini suggests wording from context you type or paste; you edit and approve

Honest limits
Recognition is experimental and often wrong. Results shown are individual recorded clips, not an accuracy rate. Audio is delivered to a virtual microphone (BlackHole) that call apps such as Google Meet can select.

Built at HackUMass XIV. Narration: ElevenLabs stock voice. TACIT's own voice output: the macOS system voice.
