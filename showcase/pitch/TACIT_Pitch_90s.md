# TACIT — 90-second lightning pitch

**For:** a judge with almost no time, a walk-by, or a second visit. **Slides:** 01 → 03 → 04 → 06 (skip 00, 02, 05).
**Demo:** if the Mac is ready and Meet is connected, do the 25-second demo. If not, point at the real capture on slide 04
and say it's a capture, not live.
**Rule:** stay silent between mouthing and tapping Speak, then press Pause before you talk.

---

### 0:00–0:08 · No slide change needed · SILENT HOOK
*Look at the judge. Silently mouth "How are you doing?" Hold 2 s.*
> You saw me say something. You didn't hear a word.

### 0:08–0:25 · Slide 01
> For people who lose their voice, after throat-cancer surgery or on a breathing tube, the words are still there.
> They can still shape them with their lips. What's missing is the sound,
> and a conversation doesn't wait for you to write it down.

### 0:25–0:40 · Slide 03
> TACIT is a Mac app in the notch. I mouth a sentence, TACIT reads my lips locally, I check the words,
> and one tap speaks them in my chosen voice, straight into a Google Meet call.

### 0:40–1:05 · Slide 04 · DEMO (25 s)
*Hand the phone over.* "You're on the call." *Start communicating. Silent:* mouth **"How are you doing?"** *→ words
appear → Speak (or Edit → Speak) → Pause.*
> That went from my lips, through my approval, into the call.

*No live setup? Say instead:* "This is a real capture: the words it read, waiting for my tap. Nothing is spoken until I
press Speak."

### 1:05–1:30 · Slide 06
> It isn't proven yet: lip reading is still hard across faces and rooms, and we haven't tested with people who've lost
> their voice. That's the next step. [BEAT]
> We aren't trying to decide what someone should say. We're trying to give them another way to be heard.

---

**Timing (measured):** 146 spoken words ≈ 58 s, plus ~25 s of silent hook and demo actions ≈ 1:25.
**If they ask one question:** "How accurate?" → "On my own held-back sentences the stock model still gets about
three words in four wrong. That's why you always review before it speaks." (Appendix A1.)
