# 90 seconds — manual advance

**0–15 s:** “TACIT helps someone keep their voice and their place in a conversation. For this demo the incoming conversation is simulated; the native workflow and outgoing audio are real.” Start A, Next. Show the exact source question and simulated badge. In local mode say explicitly that speech plays locally, not into Meet.

**15–30 s:** Respond; type “Thursday at four works for me.” “These are the user's words. Nothing is spoken automatically.”

**30–45 s:** Next. “Now the proposal changes to five while I'm preparing my answer.” Wait for the actual provider status. If Gemini succeeds: “Gemini analysed the synthetic conversation; TACIT checked its exact source references.” If fallback: “Gemini is unavailable, so this labelled local comparator handles the simple time change.”

**45–65 s:** Show Four → Five and unchanged draft. “The update doesn't rewrite my response or decide my availability.” Edit to “Five works better for me.”

**65–80 s:** Enable selected voice, press Speak. In local mode: “That ran through real TTS and the local audio route.” In Meet mode invite the listener to repeat it; only then claim remote hearing.

**80–90 s:** “The point is preserving agency as conversation moves: keep your question, keep your draft, choose what to say.” Show Stop and end.
