# TACIT · Quiet Precision

Communication workspace (Operate mode). The user’s own silently articulated words are the primary task. One intentional session activation authorizes eligible automatic speech when validated recognition is available. Camera/motion, raw hypotheses, uncertainty and actual delivery remain distinct. No clinical claims. The older reply-selection design below is preserved as historical implementation context; it is no longer the primary route.

- Charcoal surfaces, warm ivory text, pale mint for selection and primary actions. Error and simulated transport have their own labeled semantic treatments.
- Manrope carries reply text and headings; DM Sans carries controls and explanations. Both are SIL OFL 1.1; original license files ship beside self-hosted variable fonts in `public/fonts/`. `font-display: swap` and sans-serif fallbacks avoid blocking. No third-party font request.
- Tokens live in `src/styles.css`: foreground, background, panel, line, muted, mint, ink, danger, and control/panel radii. shadcn/ui Button/Card composition is adapted to these tokens, with Radix Slot and CVA. One Phosphor icon family.
- Reply targets are spacious, with visible numbers, gesture mappings, focus outlines, and text states. Most controls have 44–46px targets. Mobile stacks replies and scrolls a new proposal into view.
- Motion only follows a measured hold, actual processing, a press, or an interaction. Reduced motion preserves text/state without movement. Magic UI Border Beam was reviewed and deliberately omitted: continuous ornamental motion would distract from confirmation.
- The camera is opt-in. Local model/WASM assets and a CPU worker keep frames in the browser. Gesture strengths are experimental coefficients, not confidence or lip-reading. Neutral calibration is session-only.
- Diagnostics and simulation tools are progressively disclosed. Offline fixtures are isolated at `?preview=1`, visibly labeled, and never produce audio.
