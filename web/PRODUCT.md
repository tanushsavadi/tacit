# TACIT · continuous silent communication

The user silently articulates their own words. Once they intentionally activate Live Silent Speech for a call, experimentally validated visual speech recognition may automatically deliver eligible utterances. No per-utterance confirmation, AI reply selection, canned fallback sentences or silent language-model rewrites belong in the primary experience.

People using TACIT need clear agency, predictable privacy and immediate pause/stop controls. The workspace is an Operate surface. Quiet Precision is the existing visual identity, preserved by this change.

Current truth: real webcam/MediaPipe tracking and experimental temporal motion capture run locally. The backend does not yet expose a validated continuous visual-speech protocol. Automatic transmission remains unavailable until that contract and real reliability evidence exist. MediaPipe expression coefficients and mouth-motion windows are not word recognition. AirPods word recognition was a no-go and is not integrated.

Unseen genuinely silent human utterances, non-speech movements, incorrect transmissions, abstentions, WER and end-to-end human latency must be evaluated before unattended speech is enabled. No claims of clinical validation or remote audibility based on automated playback acknowledgments.
