# Companion source notices

TACIT's UI is original. The following small MIT source adaptations are used:

- `IslandGeometry.swift`: notch measurement from [NotchDrop, Ext+NSScreen.swift](https://github.com/Lakr233/NotchDrop/blob/e70b3d715a99e47de6eb823540282eafc7401a3a/NotchDrop/Ext%2BNSScreen.swift).
- `IslandController.swift`: nonactivating panel configuration and inactive-app editing dispatch from [Cyclop, NotchPanel.swift](https://github.com/akalikbergenov/cyclop/blob/8d1b9b2045979dc7bbe742994d1cd89d51090d65/Sources/Cyclop/Notch/NotchPanel.swift).

Coucou and CodeIsland were consulted as engineering references; no substantial source copied. BoringNotch and Atoll are visual references only; no GPL source is included. No mascots, branding or sounds reused.
